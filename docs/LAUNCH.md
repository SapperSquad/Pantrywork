## Modrinth/CurseForge description draft

> **The ore dictionary that food mods never got.**
>
> Farmer's Delight, Croptopia, and Pam's HarvestCraft 2 all tag their
> items — in three incompatible dialects that never talk to each other.
> Pantrywork bridges them: every mod's cheese counts as cheese, every dough is
> dough, and their own recipes start accepting each other's ingredients.
>
> - Croptopia's grilled cheese? Works with Pam's cheese.
> - Pam's grilled cheese & ham? Works with Croptopia butter and Farmer's
>   Delight bacon — in the same craft.
> - Playing a carnivore Origin? Dragon meat, guardian tail, and bacon are
>   all correctly edible now.
> - Modders: author recipes against `#pantrywork:food_component/protein`
>   once, and every supported food mod (and future ones following the
>   `c:foods/*` convention) just works. `PantryworkTagKeys` ships for
>   compile-time use.
>
> Zero hard dependencies. Install with any subset of supported mods.
> Supported so far: Farmer's Delight, Croptopia, Pam's HarvestCraft 2
> Food Core, Ocean's Delight, End's Delight, Origins.
